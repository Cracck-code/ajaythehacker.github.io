Rules

Primary Rules

  • Obey all of Discord's TOS (includes the 13+ age requirement).
  • Use common sense.
  • Don't be toxic to others. We want to make sure our members feel as accepted as possible, regardless of their race, religion, sexuality, etc.
  • Sensitive or inappropriate content/topics is not allowed. (GORE, NSFW, etc.)
  • Do not spam/flood any chats.
  • Self promotion is not allowed in this server.
  • Abusing soundboards in voice channels is prohibited.
  • Death threats and/or doxx threats are not tolerated.
  • Evading punishments is forbidden.

You are required to follow any instructions given by any member of staff, and they may moderate the server at their discretion if they deem necessary.

PIREP Rules

  • Flights may be on either a public server or on the PFACT network. While we encourage and recommend you fly on the PFACT network, it is not required.
  • You must act professional while flying as Southwest.
  • Do not log a flight until you have fully parked at the airport.
  • Flights must be international; domestic flights are not allowed.
  • Touch and goes are not permitted. They do not count as flights.
  • If flying on the PFACT network, include "Southwest Virtual" at the bottom of your flight plan.
  • You must cruise at or above 5000 feet.
  • Do not log a flight and put the user as someone else. Only YOU can file PIREPs for YOU.
  • When taking a screenshot of your flight from PFTracker, please ensure the following are visible in your screenshot: flight trail, callsign, and flight information (altitude, speed, heading, aircraft type).
