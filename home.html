AN AWARD-WINNING VIRTUAL AIRLINE!

Italian Airlines Virtual is a professionally managed community of flight simulation enthusiasts who want to replicate the operations of a real airline. Our main goal is to provide a realistic and engaging virtual aviation environment. We give our pilots the tools and resources needed to simulate the life of an airline pilot, from mastering complex aircraft to flying virtual routes.

Join our Discord server to get all the info and talk with us and the community!

OUR SERVICES

Structured Career Progression

Advance through the ranks, unlock new aircraft, and expand your route access. A realistic and engaging career path that rewards your dedication.

Varied and Modern Fleet

Fly a complete and meticulously maintained fleet of Italian Airlines aircraft. From initial certification to the most modern aircraft.

Extended Route Network

Operate flights on a vast realistic network of domestic and international destinations. Explore the world of Italian Airlines Virtual.
